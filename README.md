
# 🌱 AgriVision AI  
**“Making farming smarter with AI and satellite insights.”**

---

## 🚀 Elevator Pitch  
AgriVision AI helps farmers and researchers identify crops and detect early health issues using satellite imagery and AI. With a Gen-AI assistant, it provides simple, actionable advice in multiple languages, making precision agriculture accessible to everyone.

---

## 📖 About the Project  
Agriculture is the backbone of our economy, but farmers still rely on traditional methods for crop health monitoring. This often leads to late detection of diseases and low productivity. **AgriVision AI** was inspired by the idea of bringing advanced AI to rural farming in a simple, affordable way.  

The platform uses **satellite imagery and AI models** to identify crop types and detect early health issues like nutrient deficiencies, pest attacks, or water stress. A built-in **Generative AI assistant** then explains these insights in simple language, even in **regional languages**, so farmers can take immediate action without technical confusion.  

Our goal? To make **precision farming accessible for every farmer**, no matter their location or education level.

---

## 💡 Inspiration  
During discussions in college, we realized that while AI is transforming industries, agriculture—one of the most important sectors—still faces huge gaps in technology adoption. Many farmers struggle because they lack timely information about their crops. We wanted to bridge that gap with something practical, tech-driven, and easy to use.

---

## 🧠 What We Learned  
- How satellite imagery can be processed to extract useful agricultural insights.  
- Basics of crop health indices like **NDVI (Normalized Difference Vegetation Index)**.  
- Building conversational AI systems that explain technical data in natural, simple language.  
- Importance of **UX for non-tech users**, especially in rural contexts.

---

## 🔨 How We Planned to Build It  
- **Step 1:** Collect and preprocess satellite images using open datasets (e.g., Sentinel, Landsat).  
- **Step 2:** Train an AI model to classify crop types and detect stress indicators.  
- **Step 3:** Integrate a Generative AI assistant for multi-language explanations and recommendations.  
- **Step 4:** Build a simple web app using **Streamlit** for easy access.  

---

## ⚡ Challenges We Faced  
- Finding **high-quality satellite image datasets** for training.  
- Understanding **remote sensing indices** and their application in agriculture.  
- Designing a system that is both **accurate and easy to use** for farmers with minimal tech experience.  
- Ensuring **multi-language support** for diverse user groups.  

---

## ✅ Features (Planned)  
- Crop identification using satellite images.  
- Early detection of crop stress (pests, water issues, nutrient deficiencies).  
- AI-based actionable recommendations.  
- Multi-language support for farmers.  

---

## 🔮 Future Scope  
- Integration with **IoT soil sensors** for real-time data.  
- Predictive analytics for **yield estimation**.  
- Mobile app version for easy accessibility in rural areas.
